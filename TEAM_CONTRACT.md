# Team Contract

**Team:** Team 1 — EMNIST Handwritten Mileage-Log Reader  
**Instructor:** Dr. Hemang Subramanian  
**Course item:** Mid-Term 2, Part 2  
**Effective date:** September 29, 2026

## Purpose

We, the members of Team 1, agree to the following commitments for the duration of the semester project. This contract defines how we meet, which tools we use, and each member's role and responsibilities.

## 1. Time to meet

- **Weekly sync:** Monday, 7:30–8:30 PM. Review the previous week, plan the coming week, and groom the backlog.
- **Weekly stand-up:** Friday, 7:30–7:45 PM. Give a brief done / doing / blockers update.
- **Response times:** Messages are answered within 12 hours. During an active sprint, blockers posted in the WhatsApp group chat are acknowledged within 4 hours.

## 2. Meeting tools

- **Synchronous meetings:** Microsoft Teams for the weekly sync and Thursday stand-up, using one recurring meeting link shared with all members.
- **Scheduling:** The Scrum Lead owns recurring Teams invites. Changes are proposed in the WhatsApp group chat and confirmed by an updated invite at least 12 hours ahead.

## 3. Collaboration tools

| Purpose | Tool and agreement |
|---|---|
| Day-to-day chat | WhatsApp group chat for communication, blockers, and quick decisions. |
| Documents | OneDrive shared project folder for report drafts, meeting notes, design documents, and the signed contract. |
| Code | Private GitHub repository; changes go through pull requests with at least one peer review before merge. |
| Tracking | JIRA Scrum board is the single source of truth for the backlog, sprints, and task status. |

## 4. Team organization and roles

| Member | Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Ishti Boedjawan | Scrum Lead | Runs stand-ups and sprint ceremonies, maintains the JIRA board, tracks progress, and removes blockers so the team keeps its cadence. |
| Ana Perez | Product Lead | Owns product vision and backlog priority; defines what it means to read a log correctly; decides which reads may auto-post and which go to a clerk; prioritizes features. |
| Felix Belevan | QA | Writes test cases and acceptance criteria; validates character-, field-, and row-level recognition accuracy; signs off before a story is marked Done. |
| Christopher Martin | Engineer | Builds the model and application: form registration, cell extraction, training, inference, validation rules, and reading interface. |
| Giuseppe Laricchia | Data Workflow | Owns the data pipeline: EMNIST loading (including transposed-image correction and class imbalance), synthetic mileage-log generator, preprocessing, splits, and reproducible data flow. |

## 5. Shared commitments

We commit to contributing our fair share, communicating early when we fall behind, keeping JIRA current, and reviewing each other's work respectfully.

Decisions are made by consensus. If the team cannot agree, the Product Lead decides on scope and the Scrum Lead decides on process. Persistent issues are raised with the instructor.
